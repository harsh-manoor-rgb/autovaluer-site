What it is

A valuation lab, in plain language

A company is worth the cash it will produce in the future, adjusted for the fact that money tomorrow is worth less than money today. That idea is called a discounted cash flow (DCF). AutoValuer does the maths for you, then asks the harder questions: what if growth is lower, what is the market already assuming, and how does the answer compare with similar companies?

Key terms in 30 seconds

The words you will see

DCF

Valuing a company by adding up its future cash flows, discounted to today's money.

WACC / discount rate

The return investors demand. A higher rate means a lower value today.

Terminal growth

The steady growth assumed forever after the first 5 years.

Intrinsic value

The model's estimate of what one share is worth, based on your assumptions.

Reverse DCF

Asks what growth the current price needs, instead of what the stock is worth.

Monte Carlo

Thousands of random what-if runs that show a range of outcomes, not one number.

Before you start

Six things worth knowing

  • It is an estimate, not advice. Results depend heavily on the assumptions you choose.
  • Look for the data badge. ● LIVE DATA means inputs came from Yahoo Finance. ● PRESET DATA means some figures are built-in defaults to check.
  • Small changes matter. Growth, discount rate and terminal growth move the answer a lot. That is what the risk tabs show.
  • Tickers use Yahoo Finance format. Examples: AAPL, MSFT, RELIANCE.NS, HDFCBANK.NS.
  • Units differ by market. US stocks are in dollars and millions. Indian stocks (.NS / .BO) are in rupees and crore.
  • The first load can be slow. The free server sleeps when unused and may need about a minute to wake.
FAQ

Common questions

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free, and there is no sign-up or login.

Is the data real-time?

Prices and financials come from Yahoo Finance and may be delayed or incomplete. The app tells you which inputs are live and which are presets.

Why does it say a famous company is overvalued?

A DCF reflects only the growth and discount-rate assumptions you set. The Reverse DCF tab shows what growth the market price is assuming, which often explains the gap.

How do I value an Indian stock?

Type the ticker with .NS (NSE) or .BO (BSE), for example RELIANCE.NS. Amounts switch to rupees and crore. For the most careful use, enter an Indian risk-free rate in the CAPM options.

Can I share a valuation?

Yes. Open the "Share this valuation" box in the terminal to copy a link that opens a specific company directly.
